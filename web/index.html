✓ The DisasterX Solution

One AI model. Four atmospheric signals. Hours of extra warning.

DisasterX ingests live satellite, reanalysis and terrain data onto a unified spatiotemporal grid, then runs it through a multi-task deep learning engine with dedicated prediction heads for each hazard type.

🧠 Multi-Task Deep Learning

Three hazards, predicted simultaneously — not in isolation.

A shared spatiotemporal cross-attention backbone learns the relationships between moisture, energy, and terrain, then feeds three specialized output heads that each predict a different hazard at once, at every grid cell, every few minutes.

✓Cloudburst Head — flags explosive moisture pooling before rain intensifies.
✓Flash Flood Head — fuses rainfall potential with slope & drainage to predict where water will funnel.
✓Severe Thunderstorm Head — tracks instability energy and wind shear for lightning & squall risk.
🔍 Explainable AI (XAI)

Never a black-box alert. Every warning shows its evidence.

Emergency responders can't act on a number alone — they need to know why. Click any point on the live map and DisasterX breaks down the exact percentage contribution of each atmospheric precursor driving that risk score, in plain language.

✓Live sensor readings for IWV, CAPE, cloud-top cooling rate, and terrain slope at any coordinate.
✓Auto-generated plain-language explanation of the dominant driving factor.
✓Actionable SOP advisories for SDRF / NDRF field teams — not just raw probabilities.
The 4 Atmospheric Precursors

The physical signals DisasterX watches, second by second.

01
💧
Moisture Fuel (IWV)
Integrated Water Vapor from INSAT‑3DR. Rapid surges above 60 kg/m² signal explosive moisture pooling — the fuel for a cloudburst.
02
⚡
Instability & Energy
CAPE surge and CIN erosion from IMDAA baselines. Values above 2,500 J/kg indicate severe thunderstorm energy is loaded.
03
🌪️
Kinematics & Updrafts
Explosive Cloud Top Temperature drop rate and low-level wind convergence — the trigger that confirms a violent updraft is forming.
04
⛰️
Topographic Catalyst
Fuses cloudburst vectors with CartoDEM/SRTM slope & river-basin runoff routing to predict exactly where flash floods will strike.
From Raw Data to Actionable Warning

How a warning reaches the ground in minutes.

A continuous four-stage pipeline — running every few minutes, every day, across every monitored sector.

01

Ingest Live Data

Satellite moisture channels, reanalysis thermodynamics, and live ground observations are pulled and fused onto a unified spatial grid.

02

Run the MTL Engine

The spatiotemporal multi-task model scores every grid cell for cloudburst, flash flood, and thunderstorm probability, out to a 6-hour horizon.

03

Explain the Risk

Threshold breaches trigger the XAI engine, which attributes the exact precursor mix behind each alert in human-readable form.

04

Dispatch the Alert

Categorized warnings with action advisories are pushed to the live map, mesh-network mobile clients, and responder webhooks.
