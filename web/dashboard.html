How DisasterX Works & User Guide

1. The Core Objective

DisasterX is an AI Early Warning engine that predicts Cloudbursts, Severe Thunderstorms, and Flash Floods 2 to 6 hours before impact, bypassing traditional weather simulation delays.

2. The 4 Key Atmospheric Precursors

💧 Integrated Water Vapor (IWV)

The Fuel: Sensed by INSAT-3DR satellite. Rapid surges above 60 kg/m² indicate explosive moisture pooling for cloudbursts.

⚡ CAPE / Instability

The Energy: Convective Available Potential Energy from IMDAA. Values above 2500 J/kg indicate severe thunderstorm energy.

🌪️ Cloud Top Cooling Rate

The Trigger: Thermal IR drop rate. Rapid cooling > 12 K/hr confirms explosive vertical updrafts inside the storm.

⛰️ DEM Topographic Drainage

The Flood Catalyst: High-resolution slope and river catchments calculate where cloudburst rain will funnel into flash floods.

3. How to Interact with the Dashboard

  • Timeline Controller (Sidebar): Click +2h, +3h, +4h, +5h, +6h or press ▶ Play to watch how the risk evolves in time.
  • Layer Switcher (Sidebar): Filter by Cloudburst, Flash Flood, Thunderstorm, or Moisture Fuel (IWV).
  • Click Any Map Point: Opens the XAI Diagnostics panel showing the exact % breakdown of why an alert was triggered.
  • Warnings Button (🔔): View categorized emergency advisories for SDRF/NDRF teams.
  • Test Simulation (⚡): Injects an instant localized convective storm scenario for live demonstration.
